# Superstore Performance Analytics & Business Intelligence Project

## Project Overview

The Superstore dataset contains historical sales transactions across multiple products, regions, customer segments, and shipping methods.

The goal of this project is to build a Business Intelligence and Analytics solution that combines descriptive analytics, statistical analysis, machine learning, and interactive dashboarding to help stakeholders make evidence-based business decisions.

The project will move beyond traditional reporting by validating business assumptions using statistical tests and predictive models.

---

# Business Objectives

## Strategic Objectives

1. Understand the relationship between discounting and profitability.
2. Identify high-performing and underperforming regions.
3. Determine which product categories drive profitability.
4. Evaluate customer segment performance.
5. Assess the financial impact of different shipping methods.
6. Identify statistically significant drivers of profit.
7. Predict profitability using machine learning.
8. Identify customer groups through segmentation analysis.

---

# Business Questions

## Discount Strategy

- Does discounting increase profitability?
- At what discount level does profitability deteriorate?
- Is the relationship between discount and profit statistically significant?

## Regional Performance

- Which regions generate the highest profit?
- Which regions need improvement?

## Product Managers

Interested in product portfolio performance.

Questions:
- Which categories contribute the most profit?
- Which products are loss-making?

## Operations Managers

Interested in shipping efficiency.

Questions:
- Which shipping modes produce the best outcomes?
- Are expedited shipping options reducing profit?

---

# Scope

The dashboard will analyse:

- Sales
- Profit
- Discount
- Category
- Sub-Category
- Region
- Customer Segment
- Ship Mode

The dashboard will not analyse:

- Inventory levels
- Supplier performance
- Forecasting
- Customer churn

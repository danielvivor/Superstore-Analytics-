# Superstore Performance Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

The Superstore dataset contains historical sales transactions across
multiple products, regions, customer segments, and shipping methods.

The goal of this project is to build an analytics dashboard that helps
business stakeholders identify opportunities to improve profitability,
reduce losses, and optimize operational performance.

---

# Business Objectives

1. Understand the relationship between discounting and profitability.
2. Identify high-performing and underperforming regions.
3. Determine which product categories drive profit.
4. Evaluate customer segment profitability.
5. Assess the impact of shipping methods on financial performance.

---

# Stakeholders

## Executive Leadership

Interested in overall business health and strategic decisions.

Questions:
- Are we profitable?
- Which areas require intervention?
- Where should investment be focused?

## Sales Management

Interested in pricing and discount strategy.

Questions:
- Do discounts increase profit?
- Which products are frequently sold at a loss?

## Regional Managers

Interested in geographic performance.

Questions:
- Which regions generate the highest profit?
- Which regions need improvement?

## Product Managers

Interested in product portfolio performance.

Questions:
- Which categories contribute the most profit?
- Which products are loss-making?

## Operations Managers

Interested in shipping efficiency.

Questions:
- Which shipping modes produce the best outcomes?
- Are expedited shipping options reducing profit?

---

# Scope

The dashboard will analyse:

- Sales
- Profit
- Discount
- Category
- Sub-Category
- Region
- Customer Segment
- Ship Mode

The dashboard will not analyse:

- Inventory levels
- Supplier performance
- Forecasting
- Customer churn

---

# Expected Deliverables

- Streamlit dashboard
- Executive summary page
- Five analytical dashboard sections
- Visualisations and KPI cards
- Business recommendations

---

# Success Criteria

The solution should enable users to:

- Identify loss-making products
- Compare regional profitability
- Compare customer segments
- Evaluate shipping effectiveness

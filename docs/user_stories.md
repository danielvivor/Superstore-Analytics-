### **Business Problem**

Management suspects that aggressive discounting increases sales but may be destroying profit.

### **User Story 1**

As a Sales Director, I want to understand how discount levels impact profitability so that I can identify discount practices that reduce company earnings.

### **Acceptance Criteria**
- [ ] Discount percentages are grouped into meaningful bands.
- [ ] Average profit is calculated for each discount band.
- [ ] Visualisation clearly shows discount versus profit relationship.
- [ ] Products with high discounts and negative profits are identifiable.
- [ ] Dashboard highlights the discount threshold where profitability deteriorates.

### **Tasks**
_Data Analysis_
- [ ] Calculate profit by discount band.
- [ ] Calculate average discount per product.
- [ ] Aggregate sales and profit metrics.

_Visualisation_
- [ ] Create Discount vs Profit scatter plot.
- [ ] Create Profit by Discount Band chart.
- [ ] Highlight loss-making products.

_Dashboard_
- [ ] Add Discount Analysis section.
- [ ] Add KPI cards:
- [ ] Average Discount
- [ ] Total Profit
- [ ] Loss-Making Orders

_Executive Insight_
- [ ] Generate recommendation on discount policy.
